## 2. Route-by-Route Summary of Findings

- **Homepage (`/`):** Strongest layout, but overly repetitive 4-column/3-column/5-column rounded-2xl glass grids and `01 //` AI eyebrows; Hero card white-box contrast feels pasted-in; 2nd-row floating pills clutter the header.
- **Catalogue (`/products`):** Filter sidebar works well, but grid cards lack scannable spec attributes and repeat 3-button vertical stacks identically across 12 items; mobile filter bar needs collapsible drawer so products appear immediately above the fold on 375px.
- **Product Detail (`/products/[slug]`):** Spec table and enquiry form are functional, but layout lacks a sticky right-hand **Order Request Buy Box** (Amazon-inspired clarity: price, stock status, qty, `Add to Cart`, `Proceed to Order Request`, `WhatsApp Enquiry` grouped cleanly above the fold).
- **Refurbished (`/refurbished` & `[slug]`):** Good data transparency (`deviceCode`, `conditionGrade`, `batteryHealthPct`, `inspection` report), but cards look identical to `/products` instead of emphasizing hardware spec-sheet density.
- **Services & Sell Laptop (`/services`, `/sell-laptop`):** Functional forms, but visual rhythm matches every other card grid; booking form sits far down below 6 tall cards on `/services` (needs anchor/summary bar).
- **Contact & About (`/contact`, `/about`):** Cleanly fixed in 5C for dark-mode contrast and placeholder removal, but can present store capabilities and quick-action routing more concisely without empty-feeling 3-card filler when `address`/`phone`/`email` are unpublished.
- **Cart (`/cart`), Auth (`/login`, `/register`, `/verify-otp`), & Customer Portal (`/account/*`):** Clean contrast after 5C, but visual spacing/borders match generic forms; `/cart` benefits from a clearer 2-step **Order Request** summary rail and empty-state product links.
